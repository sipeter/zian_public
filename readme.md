# Zian - Local-First Offline Voice Input

> Keep your voice on your own device.
>
> Speak directly into any text field, or use your phone as a wireless microphone for your PC.

## Introduction

Zian ([ziansoft.com](https://ziansoft.com)) is a local voice input and transcription tool for Windows.

Speech recognition runs on your PC without relying on a cloud speech-recognition service. Use Zian to write documents, chat, enter code, or transcribe longer audio and video files.

ZianLink for Android and iOS can also connect to your PC over the local network and turn your phone into a wireless microphone.

## Features

- **Local voice input**: Hold `CapsLock` by default, speak, and release to enter text. The hotkey can be customized.
- **Two local recognition engines**: Use SenseVoice by default or install FunASR-Nano when needed. CPU and NVIDIA GPU editions are available.
- **Correction and local rules**: Correct recent input, save recurring changes as local rules, and quickly recall the latest sentence.
- **Zian Scribe**: Transcribe audio and video files with batch processing, progress display, preview, and export.
- **ZianLink phone microphone**: Use an Android phone or iPhone as a PC microphone over your LAN. Android supports both Hold and Tap recording modes.
- **Flexible text output**: Choose clipboard paste or simulated keystrokes for different editors, chat apps, and games.
- **Optional text cleanup**: Use punctuation processing, custom rules, and AI-assisted polishing when needed.
- **User-initiated updates**: Zian checks for updates only when you ask it to; it does not silently check in the background.

## Privacy

Zian's speech-recognition core runs locally and does not upload your recordings or transcripts for cloud recognition.

License activation, user-initiated update checks, and optional model downloads may access the internet. Phone-microphone audio travels over your local network and is processed by the models on your PC.

## Platform Support

### Windows

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- CPU and NVIDIA GPU editions
- Installer and portable packages
- 8 GB RAM or more recommended
- .NET Framework 4.7.2 or later

### Android - ZianLink

- Use an Android phone as a wireless microphone for Zian on PC
- Hold and Tap recording modes
- Google Play and APK distribution

### iOS - ZianLink

- Use an iPhone as a wireless microphone for Zian on PC
- Recording control and input-completion feedback
- The IPA must be signed before installation

## Quick Start

1. Download the CPU or GPU edition for your PC.
2. Run the installer, or extract the portable package to a path without special characters.
3. Start `Zian.exe` and wait for the tray icon to appear.
4. Focus a text field, hold `CapsLock`, speak, and release to enter the result.
5. To use a phone microphone, enable ZianLink on the PC and connect from your phone.
